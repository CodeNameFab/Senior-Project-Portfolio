About

Two halves that belong together

Most people I meet in this field are strong on the technical side or strong with people. I have spent my time at Wichita State refusing to pick one. I build systems that measure things nobody was measuring, and I lead the organizations and communities I belong to. This portfolio puts both in one place, because the combination is the point.

I also started a business from nothing and ran it for four years. That shaped how I work more than any class did: own the whole thing, finish it, and be accountable for the result.

Build it and own it end to end

Whether it was founding G&G from nothing or designing an AI governance platform on a blank slate, I am drawn to creating something complete and being fully responsible for it, not just maintaining what already exists.

Leadership and service are the thread

Four of the five things I am proudest of are about strengthening a community. What makes me proud is not achievement in isolation, it is the impact on the people around me.

Earn the responsibility back

Asked to serve a second term, selected for a competitive scholarship, recognized at two levels in the same year. The pattern is the same: given responsibility, deliver, given more.

Burns & McDonnell  /  Business Technology Intern  /  Chicago  /  Summer 2026

Room Resource Intelligence Platform

Conference room data lived in six enterprise systems that disagreed with each other, which meant the firm was paying for licenses on rooms nobody could account for. I built an automated pipeline that reconciled all six into one governed master register and scored each room for disposition, then reported it through Power BI.

  • 1,766Records reconciled
  • 6Enterprise sources
  • $38KYearly recoverable spend

Microsoft Fabric  ·  Power BI  ·  Python  ·  Microsoft Graph API

ReflectionReconciling six systems taught me that the hard part of data work is rarely the pipeline. It is deciding which source to trust, and being able to defend that decision to the people who own each system.

Burns & McDonnell  /  Business Technology Intern  /  Kansas City  /  Summer 2025

Microsoft 365 Alert Audit and Support Analytics

Microsoft announced it was retiring SharePoint alerts, which meant the firm needed to know how many it had and where they lived before they quietly stopped working. I led the initiative to find out, auditing every existing alert across the organization with the Microsoft 365 Assessment Tool and reporting back on what had to be replaced and where the gaps were.

In the same role I built a Power BI dashboard for the Microsoft 365 support team based in India, surfacing long term performance trends and operational bottlenecks so leadership could act on the specific challenges of running a support team spread across the world.

Microsoft 365 Assessment Tool  ·  Azure app registration  ·  Microsoft Graph API  ·  SharePoint API  ·  Certificate-based authentication  ·  Power BI

ReflectionMy first internship, and the first time my work had to be useful to people on the other side of the world. Standing up certificate based authentication against a live enterprise tenant taught me that in a real environment access is never the easy part. It is the part you plan for first.
